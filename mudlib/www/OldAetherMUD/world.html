World

Status: live = playable; in progress = small / growing; planned = not in game. Room counts from docs/ASSESSMENT.md.

Travel

  • Starting zones (chargen): Chi-Town (Americas), Splynn (Atlantis), New Camelot (Europe).
  • Rift: rift <destination>, pay universal credits, enter before the portal closes. Others in the room can follow through. Moxim NPCs in Chi-Town, Splynn, New Camelot, Tolkeen, Lazlo, and Praxis.
  • Hovertrain: Chi-Town Burbs <-> Horton.

Americas

Chi-Town live

  • Americas start. Coalition States capital; fortified city + Burbs.
  • Built: shopping arcade, ISS checkpoint plaza, residential maintenance hatches, sewers.
  • Dog Boys / Psi-Stalkers at checkpoints; SAMAS present.

Praxis live

  • Hub town (not a chargen start). Reachable by rift.
  • Bank, market, hospital (cyber-doc), sheriff, library, pubs, bounty board, Moxim.
  • Nearby: catacombs, cemetery, mountain trails, Coalition territory.

Tolkeen live

  • Magic city under CS siege. Liberation Square (rift master), mage quarter, market, inn, siege fields, warded crypt.

Horton live

  • Human town. Access: hovertrain from Chi-Town Burbs (or long overland).
  • Wilderness: bandit camps, flooded quarry, wolf dens, ley-line glade.

Lone Star in progress

  • CS genetics / mutant research. Containment wing + subjects built; complex still expanding.

Puerto Angel in progress

  • Vampire coast town. Docks (dockmaster), beach. Small zone.

Lazlo in progress

  • Magic / learning city. Outpost only so far. Moxim present.
