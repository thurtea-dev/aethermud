Admin

Position: arch / head arch. Role tag: admin.

Tools

  • Staff of Demotion: promote, demote
  • Staff of Dominion: domain (includes apprentice submissions)
  • Staff of Creation: create, modify, add, delete, about, more, clone, purge, review
  • RP Skill Tool and Tattoo Gun
  • Ring of Dominion: grants the five tools above while worn; askring <question>

Staff toolkit · Admin / creator commands

Promotion

makewiz <player>
setrole <player> admin|domain|coding|rp
  • makewiz - Apprentice + kit
  • setrole - permanent role after track task is ready

Reload

  • warmboot - daemons, cmds, obj, /domains/adm; notifies online admins
  • ./mud.sh restart - /std/ or simul-efun changes
